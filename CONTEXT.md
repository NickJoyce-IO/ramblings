# Ramblings

A personal blog by Nick Joyce, Head of Data and Development in UK Government, about applying AI-assisted development inside the constraints of the public sector. It is also the vehicle for getting hands-on with code again.

## Language

**Ramblings**:
The working name of the blog. It may change; the domain is the lasting brand.
_Avoid_: the site, the app

**Post**:
A single published piece of writing on Ramblings. Every Post is exactly one of the three kinds below.
_Avoid_: article, entry, page

**Essay**:
A Post giving an opinion or argument about AI-assisted delivery in government.
_Avoid_: opinion piece, long-form

**Build Log**:
A Post recording how Ramblings itself is being built with agents, including decisions and mistakes.
_Avoid_: devlog, diary, tutorial

**Note**:
A short Post capturing one small learning.
_Avoid_: snippet, micro-post, tip

**Series**:
A named, ordered group of Posts on one theme (for example the Build Log posts).
_Avoid_: category, collection, tag

**Publishing Checklist**:
The fixed set of checks every Post must pass before publication, ensuring nothing non-public or departmental is disclosed.
_Avoid_: review, sign-off

**How This Is Made**:
The page that states plainly where and how AI was used in producing Ramblings.
_Avoid_: AI disclaimer, about page

## Relationships

- A Post is an Essay, a Build Log, or a Note, and may belong to one Series.
- No Post is published without passing the Publishing Checklist.
